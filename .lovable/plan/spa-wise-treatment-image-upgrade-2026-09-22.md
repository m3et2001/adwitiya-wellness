# Spa-wise Treatment Image Upgrade

## Goal
Give every treatment card across Ganga Spa, Amore Wellness, and Sattva Wellness an image that visually matches that treatment’s name while retaining each spa’s own identity.

## Changes
- Replace the current repeated image on every treatment card with treatment-specific massage and therapy imagery.
- Create separate visual sets for each spa:
  - **Ganga Spa:** forest green, sage, timeless and serene.
  - **Amore Wellness:** champagne beige, warm gold accents, contemporary and refined.
  - **Sattva Wellness:** sage, earthy neutrals, balanced and mindful.
- Match specialized services accurately, including Foot Reflexology, Four Hand Massage, water therapies, hammams, aromatherapy, chocolate massage, and Sattva packages.
- Keep all imagery premium, tasteful, modestly draped, calm, and consistent in landscape composition.
- Preserve current treatment names, categories, descriptions, pending price/duration labels, and booking actions.

## Technical details
- Generate and assign one relevant image per treatment card rather than sharing one image across a spa.
- Keep the treatment data-driven structure and existing reusable spa page.
- Add descriptive image text for accessibility.
- Verify all three spa pages on desktop and mobile for correct image assignment, loading, card layout, and overflow.
