# ADWITYA WELLNESS premium website rebuild

## What will change
- Rebuild the homepage around the new ADWITYA WELLNESS journey: cinematic hero, three spa destinations, Philosophy, Reason, Motto, and a refined contact-focused footer.
- Replace the current navigation with Home, Our Spas, Our Philosophy, Our Reason, Our Motto, Contact, plus a prominent booking action and mobile menu.
- Create one reusable, data-driven spa detail experience for Ganga Spa, Amore Wellness, and Sattva Wellness so future content updates do not require redesigning pages.
- Add complete spa-page sections: full-screen intro, about, treatment pricing, clearly marked demo location/map, demo reviews, contact details, photo lightbox, demo Instagram grid, reel-style video placeholder, and booking actions.
- Add a spa-specific booking panel with demo Call and WhatsApp links, plus an elegant mobile bottom action bar.

## Visual direction
- Use a cinematic editorial hospitality style: warm ivory space, deep earthy green, muted brown, charcoal, and restrained gold accents.
- Keep layouts varied through full-width imagery, asymmetrical editorial compositions, refined lists, selective cards, large typography, and generous breathing room.
- Preserve the uploaded logo as the visual identity while standardizing the requested spelling, ADWITYA WELLNESS, throughout visible copy and metadata.
- Use subtle image zoom, scroll reveal, button movement, and header transitions, with reduced-motion support.

## Demo-content system
- Centralize every spa field—name, tagline, description, imagery, treatments, location, phone, email, hours, social handle, map link, reviews, and reel—in typed shared data.
- Label demo addresses, contact details, social content, pricing, reviews, map previews, and media clearly so visitors cannot mistake them for verified business information.
- Use only project-owned generated/demo imagery; no hotlinked media or claims about real locations.

## Responsive and accessible behavior
- Optimize desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts with 44px minimum controls, no horizontal overflow, responsive media, swipe-friendly mobile galleries, and a non-obstructive sticky spa action bar.
- Add keyboard-friendly dialogs and lightboxes, visible focus states, semantic headings, descriptive image text, and readable overlays.

## Verification
- Check every content page for unique title, description, social metadata, and correct heading hierarchy.
- Test homepage navigation, spa cards, all three spa pages, gallery lightbox, booking panel, Call/WhatsApp links, mobile menu, sticky actions, scrolling, and desktop/mobile overflow in the live preview.
